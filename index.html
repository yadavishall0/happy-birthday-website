A Final Confession

My Last Letter to You

Happy Birthday. ❤️

Well… this is my last confession to you.

I know I sometimes get angry when you joke or talk about other guys. It isn't because I hate anyone or want to control you. It is because I love you so deeply and admire you so much that even something as small as hearing about someone else hurts me.

I know you have thousands of reasons not to commit to me, and I understand your situation. I know accepting my love isn't easy for you. But you should understand mine too.

Among all those reasons and all those doubts, there is still one thing that challenges them all—my love for you.

I don't know how to stop this obsession. I have tried to understand it, but I still don't know how to simply stop loving you.

I never wanted to force you to be with me. I only wanted you to accept me if you truly loved me and were ready to make me your life partner.

So from now, I will try to find a way to end this obsession and learn to live without constantly reaching for you.

And because I understand your situation, I want you to understand mine too:

Please don't call or message me again—not even on my birthday—unless you truly love me and are ready for a commitment.

Because I don't want you in my life halfway.

I want you to be my whole life.

I may still want your call. I may still wait for it. But I won't keep chasing you.

I will step away and let time decide.

And somewhere inside, I still hope that one day you will find the courage to come back and make me your life partner.

Until then, I won't force anything.

So let's see who wins in the end—

my love,
or your confusions.

Whatever happens, I want you to know that what I felt for you was real.
